\begin{abstract}
This document traces the arc of \emph{Il Portale}, a concept for a civic music
learning point first proposed between 2013 and 2016 as a submission to the
Free Technology Guild journal.  The original proposal combined a physical
drop-shaped audio---visual structure, a digital keyboard, an OLED display,
and networked learning resources into a single accessible station for music
education.  The present work reconstructs the historical concept, situates it
within the peeragogical framework that informed its design, and documents the
ongoing exploration of the Tea Pavilion in Parco Caprotti, Bergamo, as a
candidate physical host.  No experimental results are reported; this is a
position and research-through-design document written to scaffold collaborative
development and critical review.
\end{abstract}
