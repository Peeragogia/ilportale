\section{Future Work}\label{sec:future-work}

The following directions are identified for the next stages of this project.
None are executed yet; they are listed here to frame the editorial roadmap.

\paragraph*{Blender reconstruction and spatial design.}
A proportional blockout of the Tea Pavilion exists (Blender 5.2, 215 objects,
5 cameras).  The next step is to fix the interior lighting, pass the quality
gate (80/100), and test three placement hypotheses for Il Portale inside the
pavilion.  The Blender workflow is non-destructive and versioned.

\paragraph*{Learning experience design.}
The physical interaction model---seated versus standing, one-user versus
multi-user, headphones versus open audio, screen visibility from outside---is
unresolved.  Each choice affects the spatial arrangement and the perceived
relationship between historical architecture and contemporary intervention.

\paragraph*{Evaluation.}
No user testing or formal evaluation has been conducted.  Criteria for
evaluating the prototype (usability, accessibility, learning outcomes,
civic fit) are not yet defined.

\paragraph*{Publication and peer review.}
This document is a scaffold.  The next editorial cycle will involve
collaborative revision with ChatGPT and critical review by Fabrizio Terzi
before any public release.

\paragraph*{Open questions.}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Exact external dimensions of the pavilion (field measurement
        pending).
    \item Interior ceiling height, floor build-up, wall construction.
    \item Conservation constraints for fixing equipment to historic surfaces.
    \item Feasibility of a permanent installation versus a periodic/temporary
        setup.
    \item Integration with existing park infrastructure (power, network,
        accessibility).
\end{itemize}
