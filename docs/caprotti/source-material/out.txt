                                            Abstract

   The PEERAGOGY journal intend to act as on-line editorial open space to
   contributions from scholars belonging systems thinking. The setting of the
   journal is intended to be explicitly inter-trans-disciplinary, and expression
   of a ”mesh” vision and attentive in identify and describe the processes of
   co-generation and recursion theory in practice learning. In addition, those
   who are doing their project goals, peeragogy in action journal is designed
   as a space dedicated to epistemology and the study of complex systems,
   with a special focus on theoretical frameworks, models, and field research
   on the specific areas of Education. The intention is to provide a point of
   reference for researchers publishing in value, and to position themselves in
   the context of the current debate science and culture. Go then specified that
   the jurnal is a monthly recap and have a frequency of publication monthly
   (or) half-yearly, and that his numbers will from time to time devoted to a
   specific theme, hosting works signed by scholars from different countries,
   albeit with a special care in giving voice to those who are the realities of
   research and experimentation about peeragogy in action on the web.
