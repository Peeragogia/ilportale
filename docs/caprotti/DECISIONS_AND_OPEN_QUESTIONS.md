# Decisions and Open Questions

## Confirmed decisions

- The Tea Pavilion is the intended architectural host for Il Portale.
- This relationship is continuous with the original project’s “Learning City Point” location at Parco Caprotti.
- The project is a civic music-learning point, not merely a rendering exercise.
- Historic architecture and contemporary intervention should remain visually distinct.
- Reconstruction must be evidence-traceable and versioned.
- The original Portale geometry remains preserved and independent.
- The pavilion should be called **neo-Renaissance** in current documentation, not Liberty, unless stronger contrary evidence appears.
- Date should be written **c. 1890–1891** until primary documentation resolves the conflict.

## Open architectural questions

- Exact construction date and architect/designer?
- Are original plans/elevations held by Comune di Bergamo or another archive?
- Exact footprint and interior clear dimensions?
- Ceiling/roof construction?
- Wall and glazing thickness?
- Exact stair geometry and terrace access?
- Which decorative elements are original/restored/replaced?
- Current electrical/data/lighting/heating/ventilation capacity?
- Conservation constraints for touching/fixing to historic surfaces?
- Real accessibility constraints and threshold level?

## Open design questions for Il Portale

- Central vs lateral placement?
- One-user or multi-user interaction?
- Seated, standing or hybrid?
- Headphones, directional speakers or shared room audio?
- How much of the 2013 physical design should remain in a 2026 implementation?
- Can the installation be fully reversible and free-standing?
- How much should the interface be visible from outside through the glass?
- How should digital content connect to learning before/after the physical visit?

## Current design hypothesis

Use **lateral/functional placement** as the first baseline because it is most likely to preserve circulation and the pavilion’s visual permeability. Compare it against a central/iconic arrangement before choosing.

This remains provisional until the interior is measured.
