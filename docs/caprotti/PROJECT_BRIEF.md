# IL PORTALE — Project Brief v0.3
## Civic music learning point inside the Tea Pavilion, Parco Caprotti, Bergamo

## Vision

**Il Portale** is a civic music learning point: a small public environment where a person can approach music, listen, explore, practise, learn from digital resources, and connect with wider educational material.

The project is intended to be **built inside the Padiglione del Tè at Parco Caprotti**. The pavilion is not a scenic backdrop. It is the architectural body that makes Il Portale spatial, civic and urban.

## Spatial concept

```text
Parco Caprotti
    ↓
Tea Pavilion
    ↓
Il Portale
    ↓
Civic music learning experience
```

The park contributes landscape, publicness, pause and accidental encounter. The pavilion creates a threshold between garden and interior. Il Portale introduces another threshold: from curiosity to musical learning.

## Design principle

The intervention should not imitate the historic architecture. The intended dialogue is:

**historic pavilion + legible contemporary learning installation**

The Portale should read as new, reversible and deliberate, while preserving the visual identity and transparency of the pavilion.

## Experience hypothesis

The user journey should be testable as:

**approach → enter → discover → listen → try → learn → continue elsewhere**

This is a design hypothesis, not a fixed answer.

## What the Blender scene must let us evaluate

- Does the Portale physically fit without obstructing circulation?
- Is the pavilion still legible as architecture rather than scenery behind a device?
- Can a user enter, stand/sit, interact and leave plausibly?
- Can a second person observe or wait without blocking the space?
- Are screen, keyboard, audio and body posture coherent?
- Is the Portale visible from the garden through the glazing in a useful way?
- Does the intervention respect the pavilion’s historic surfaces visually?
- Can the concept communicate “public music learning” without a long explanation?
- Is the proposal plausibly buildable and reversible?

## Visual language

Avoid generic sci-fi styling. The project should feel contemporary, calm, legible and technically plausible.

The strongest visual tension is already present:

- romantic garden;
- late-19th-century neo-Renaissance glazed pavilion;
- contemporary music-learning interface.

## First design milestone

A recognisable, evidence-traceable pavilion blockout containing a correctly scaled working copy of Il Portale, with enough exterior and interior views to judge spatial feasibility.

## Definition of Done — architectural concept v0.1

A viewer unfamiliar with the project should understand from the scene that a small historic pavilion in a public park is being reactivated as a contemporary civic music-learning point.
