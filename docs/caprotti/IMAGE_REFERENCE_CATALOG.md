These images are remote references for research and modelling. They are **not copied into this repository**. Always preserve the source page and attribution. Use them for visual analysis, not redistribution.

## Image coverage status

Current public references are sufficient for a **first exterior proportional blockout**. They are insufficient for:

- reliable rear elevation;
- precise roof surface geometry;
- interior plan and ceiling;
- wall thickness;
- stair dimensions;
- accurate sculpture/relief reproduction.

Fabrizio’s field photographs should therefore be used to close those gaps rather than to restart the research from zero.
